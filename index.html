Narrative

A browser-based lyrics and scripture presenter. Your songs, Bibles and settings live in this browser (IndexedDB). Use Export for backups.

Projector: the first time, Chrome asks to “manage windows on all your displays”. Allow it, and Narrative opens the output on the second screen by itself.
